HKRS Comparison — Public media library

This site serves original figures and standard PDFs. The comparison application and its independent clause quotations remain in their separate repository.

Standard PDFs

Chinese-standard links in the comparison app open the original PDF directly in your browser. Native PDF viewing and download behavior depend on your browser settings.

Example: GB 50307-2012 PDF

Upload or browse standard PDFs on GitHub

Use the exact mapped filename on the main branch. GitHub Pages republishes committed uploads; wait for deployment to complete before testing a new document. Missing files are not replaced with other standards or editions.

Privacy and rights

All files in this repository and site are public. Upload only documents and figures you are authorized to redistribute. Do not upload confidential or restricted material. This library grants no license to source standards.
